# Golf Video — Recording Brief

## What we need on video (the more variety, the better)
- **6 people** — at least 1 left-handed; ideally a mix of heights, builds, and swing speeds.
- **6 clubs** — driver, 3-wood, hybrid, 7-iron, wedge, putter.
- **4 balls** — white (most important, shoot the most), optic-yellow, striped range ball, scuffed old white.
- **5 environments** — driving range, putting green, indoor mat/net, outdoor grass, indoor floor (putting / static shots only — don't hit full swings off a hard floor).

## What to record
- **Setup / address stills (most important)** — ball and club head both sharp and fully in frame.
- **Putts** — the whole stroke plus the ball rolling (leave time to fetch the ball).
- **Full swings (driving range / net only)** — record continuously; keep the bad ones (tops, thins) too; no staged whiffs.
- **Grip, waggle, and walk-ups.**
- **Keep the camera rolling between shots** (don't keep stopping); now and then film the clutter around you (ball buckets, sprinkler heads, yardage markers, alignment sticks).
